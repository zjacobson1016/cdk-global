# Genie Space Demo — Sales Desk Manager

**Persona:** Dana, Sales Desk Manager at a multi-franchise dealership. It's Monday morning and she's reviewing the weekend's F&I pipeline before the team huddle.

---

### Question 1 — Weekend Pipeline Snapshot

> "How many loan applications came in since the beginning of the year, and what's the approval rate?"

*Sets the stage — Dana checks the volume and health of the pipeline at a glance.*

---

### Question 2 — Red Flags in Denials

> "For applications that were denied since the beginning of the year, what is the average credit score, debt-to-income ratio, and income verification ratio?"

*Dana digs into why deals are falling through so she can coach the sales team on which customers to pre-qualify more carefully.*

---

### Question 3 — Documentation Gaps

> "How many applications have incomplete documentation since beginning of the year — missing a pay stub or photo ID — and what percentage of those were denied?"

*She suspects incomplete paperwork is killing deals. This tells her whether the F&I desk needs to tighten its checklist.*

---

### Question 4 — High-Value Deals at Risk

> "Show me approved applications over $40,000 where the income verification ratio is below 0.8 or the employer was not verified."

*Dana is looking for big loans that got through but carry risk — she wants to flag these before funding falls apart.*

---

### Question 5 — Coaching Opportunity

> "What is the approval rate broken down by loan purpose, and which loan purpose has the highest average loan amount?"

*She wraps up by identifying which deal types are converting best so she can steer the afternoon's floor traffic toward higher-margin opportunities.*
